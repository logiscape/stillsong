One-line: inline message, one action, no jargon.

```jsx
<Notice tone="calm" glass title="This song wanted to run longer than expected"
  action="Let it finish">We can give it another half minute and keep everything else the same.</Notice>
<Notice tone="error" title="Something went wrong while writing your song" action="Try again">
  Nothing was lost — your photo and choices are still here.</Notice>
```

Never more than one action. Technical detail lives behind a separate quiet "details" disclosure, never in the body copy.
