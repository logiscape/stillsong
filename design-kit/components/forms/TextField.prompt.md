One-line: text entry for the quiet options on Create and every field in Remix.

```jsx
<TextField label="Genre or mood hint" placeholder="slow folk ballad" value={hint} onChange={setHint} />
<TextField label="Caption" multiline rows={5} value={caption} onChange={setCaption} />
```

Placeholders are examples, never instructions. Labels are sentence case and never end in a colon.
